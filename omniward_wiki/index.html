Plant a ward. Hold the ground. Capture theirs.

OmniWard turns any map into a contest of control points. Players plant wards that project a team aura buff to nearby allies — and enemies channel-capture them under fire to flip or destroy the objective. Fully config-driven, thread-safe, and Folia-ready.

What it does

Aura projection

Each ward continuously applies configurable PotionEffects to allied players standing within its radius. Buff your team's control point.

Channel capture

Enemies stand in range and channel for N seconds to seize the ward. The channel breaks the moment they take damage or leave range.

Flip or destroy

Choose the capture outcome: FLIP transfers control to the capturer's side, or DESTROY removes the ward entirely.

Pluggable teams

Uses Bukkit scoreboard teams when present, otherwise falls back to a simple owner-vs-everyone model. No hard team-plugin dependency.

Live feedback

Particle rings mark every ward and turn hostile while contested. Capture progress streams to action bars for attackers and defenders.

Limits & lifetime

Per-player ward caps, a server-wide total, plant cooldowns, and automatic expiry keep the map balanced.

Non-destructive markers

An optional marker block is placed on plant and the original block is restored on removal — tracked in runtime state.

Hot reload

/ward reload re-reads config.yml and safely stops and restarts the repeating region task with the new settings.

Folia-safe

All entity effect application and block edits are dispatched onto the owning region thread via FoliaLib.

How capture works

  1. A ward is planted by its owner and immediately begins projecting its aura to allies inside the radius.
  2. Every tick interval, the repeating task scans players in range and splits them into allies and enemies using the team resolver.
  3. Allies within range receive the configured aura PotionEffects; the nearest enemy in range becomes the active channeler.
  4. Each tick the channeler holds position, capture progress advances. Progress and a live bar are shown on action bars.
  5. If the channeler takes damage, or no enemy remains in range, the channel is interrupted and progress resets to zero.
  6. When progress reaches the requirement, the ward flips to the capturer's side (FLIP) or is destroyed (DESTROY). Owners are notified.

Installation

  1. Drop OmniWard-x.y.z.jar into your server's plugins/ folder.
  2. Start the server once to generate plugins/OmniWard/config.yml.
  3. Tune the radius, aura effects and capture timing, then run /ward reload.

Optional soft dependencies

  • Vault — detected if present; OmniWard degrades gracefully without it.
  • PlaceholderAPI — detected if present; no hard dependency.

Teams

If the ward owner belongs to a Bukkit scoreboard team, allies are resolved by shared team membership. Otherwise only the owner is an ally and everyone else is an enemy.

Configuration

A trimmed example — every field falls back to a sensible default if omitted.

ward:
  radius: 8.0            # aura + capture radius in blocks
  max-per-player: 2      # wards a single player may hold
  max-total: 50          # server-wide ward cap
  lifetime-seconds: 300  # 0 = permanent until captured
  cooldown-seconds: 30   # delay between plants per player

capture:
  seconds: 5             # channel time to capture
  mode: FLIP             # FLIP (transfer control) or DESTROY
  tick-interval-ticks: 10 # task cadence (20 ticks = 1s)

aura:
  duration-ticks: 40     # effect refresh window (>= tick interval x2)
  effects:               # "TYPE:level" — level is 1-based
    - "SPEED:1"
    - "REGENERATION:1"
    - "DAMAGE_RESISTANCE:1"

visual:
  place-block: true      # place a marker block on plant
  marker-block: BEACON   # restored to original on removal
  owner-particle: HAPPY_VILLAGER
  capture-particle: ANGRY_VILLAGER
  particle-points: 24    # ring resolution

messages:
  planted: "&aWard planted! It projects an aura within &e%s&a blocks."
  captured-flip: "&aYou captured the ward! It now serves your side."
  captured-destroy: "&aYou destroyed the enemy ward!"
  capture-interrupted: "&cCapture interrupted — you took damage!"
  progress-enemy: "&aCapturing... &f%bar% &7%pct%%"
  progress-ally: "&e⌼ Ward under attack! &f%bar% &7%pct%%"
